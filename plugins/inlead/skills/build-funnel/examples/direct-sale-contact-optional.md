# Template: direct sale, contact optional

**Objective:** sell at an external checkout; the completed quiz is the lead.
**Size:** 30–40 steps. **Branching:** linear. **Contact:** none or minimal —
skipping e-mail and phone removes the highest-friction step when the sale
itself is the goal.

## Block walk-through

1. **Hook** — "your [plan] based on your age" → first interaction: age, as
   options with images. A small terms line under the options is enough.
2. **Measures** — the inputs for the projection (dedicated widgets, one per
   screen).
3. **Profiling** — lifestyle, level, target, one question per screen.
4. **Proof mid-funnel** — "based on your answers you will reach [target] in 21
   days" with a chart, plus testimonials with photos.
5. **Loading** — a social-proof count ("+N thousand people") while the result is
   prepared.
6. **Diagnosis** — the person's profile on a chart (e.g. where they stand
   today) with their variables.
7. **Sales page** (one long final step) — name at the top, benefit bullets,
   before/after metrics (today × 21 days), bonuses, the price shown first as a
   fraction ("12 × R$ X" or "R$ X a day") then in full, "one-time payment,
   lifetime access", a guarantee, urgency, two or more CTAs to the external
   checkout with PIX highlighted.

## What defines this template

- No contact form at all: the person goes from the result to the checkout.
  Use it only when the objective is a direct sale.
- Urgency and scarcity are heavy in this template; keep them only when they
  are true — a resetting timer or "last N spots" on a digital product raises
  the first conversion and destroys trust.
