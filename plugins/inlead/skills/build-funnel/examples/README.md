# Reference templates

Three reference templates, one per funnel objective, following the block
sequence of high-converting quiz funnels. Read the one closest
to the user's goal to calibrate blocks and size — then read a published
funnel of the account for the actual style.

| Template | Objective | Typical size | Branching | Contact capture | Ends at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Lead generation with late contact](lead-generation-late-contact.md) | collect qualified leads | long, 50–70 steps | by goal or profile | e-mail/phone late, framed as "to receive your plan" | offer or handoff |
| [Free evaluation with a report](free-evaluation-with-report.md) | sell after a personalized diagnosis | 20–30 steps | linear, with a yes-ladder | first name early (optional); no e-mail/phone required | offer |
| [Direct sale, contact optional](direct-sale-contact-optional.md) | sell directly at checkout | 30–40 steps | linear | none or minimal | external checkout |

These are structural templates: the copy patterns in them show *what kind* of
line goes in each block, not text to reuse. Rules and priorities live in the
skill.
