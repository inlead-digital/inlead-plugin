# Template: free evaluation with a report

**Objective:** sell after delivering a personalized result. **Size:** 20–30
steps. **Branching:** linear, with a yes-ladder before the result. **Contact:**
a first name early, with a privacy reassurance; e-mail and phone are not
required.

## Block walk-through

1. **Hook** — promise with result and timeframe ("[outcome] in up to 21 days"),
   a today × target image, and a CTA that frames the funnel as an evaluation, not
   a sale ("start your free evaluation").
2. **Authority** — the creator introduces themself (video, "let me introduce
   myself"), reinforced by a media mention. This comes before any question of
   substance.
3. **First name (optional, early)** — one field, with "your data is protected"
   next to it. It powers the personalization of everything after.
4. **Inputs** — the numbers the report is computed from (for a business
   course, current revenue and hours available; whatever the verdict needs).
5. **Yes-ladder** — one or two questions whose natural answer is "yes".
6. **Loading** — "we are personalizing your plan" (6–9 seconds), with proof
   while it loads.
7. **Result** — a report with the person's own numbers (levels, gaps,
   projections) and a verdict that qualifies them: "below target — needs
   action", "you fit the program".
8. **Offer** — header with the name and the person's numbers → today × target →
   value stack (modules and bonuses with struck prices → total → final price →
   a per-day anchor) → testimonials with photo, name and result → 7-day
   guarantee → two CTAs to the same checkout.

## What defines this template

- The whole funnel is framed as a free evaluation; the sale arrives as
  the natural next step of the verdict.
- Authority placed early is what makes the report credible.
- The shortest template: length comes only from the blocks, with no
  profiling detours.
